How this testing page works

 
    
  
  • This page is not linked from the app. It opens ReadTrail 2 inside a frame with ?sandbox=NAME in the address. In a sandbox the app saves under readtrail2__sandbox_NAME (its own browser storage and its own backup database), never syncs to an account, and shows a purple "TEST SANDBOX" tag. Real explorers saved under readtrail2 are never read or written.
    • 
  
  • The sandbox clock can be moved forward to test spaced review. It only changes time inside that sandbox.
    • 
  
  • Simulated learners answer through the app's own answer buttons logic (ans()), so the real lesson, quick check, placement and mastery code runs. Their answers come from a simple model: a chance of knowing each skill and letter, learning a little from each lesson, and guessing when they do not know.
    • 
  
  • Nothing here proves that children learn. It shows whether the rules work the way they are meant to.
